The evidence archive was collected from working Ubuntu; no stronger controlled latest recovery or immutable eMMC comparison is inferred.

The exact supplied archive was hashed, listed, safely imported through two nested layers and indexed. It supplies no live MAC feature-register dump or DMA visibility proof. Full decoded facts, omissions and source/choice distinctions are in evidence-index.json, gmac0-resolved.json and gmac-hardware-audit.md.

No new NC ABI is mandatory or implemented. Real cache invocations, cache-line-exclusive descriptors, pool/address provenance, bus/width audit and complete RX/TX integration remain unqualified. The 16-byte preparatory ring must not be used unchanged with cached memory. Raw-I/O and DMA-runtime-regression images are absent, not placeholders. No kernel/runtime mapping change was made.
